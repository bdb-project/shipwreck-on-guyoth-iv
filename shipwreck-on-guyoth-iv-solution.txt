Shipwreck on Guyoth IV
By Gianluca Girelli
For Z-code interpreters

Solution by Garry Francis
----------------------------------------
This is a minimal solution. You'll need to EXAMINE lots of things for this solution to make sense. Some optional (but significant) commands are shown in parentheses. These can be omitted once you know what to do.

(I) (you're wearing a harness, pressure suit and pressure helmet)
(EXAMINE HARNESS) (connects static lines to canopy and has quick release device)
(X DEVICE) (when pushed, it disconnects the static lines)
(EXAMINE SURVIVAL KIT) (it's connected by a cord)
(GET IT) (you can't reach it as it hangs well below you)
(PULL CORD) (you can't reach it, but you can untie it)
UNTIE CORD (survival kit falls into mist, but now you won't get tangled in tree)
PRESS DEVICE (you fall to the ground below)
(X MIST) (you can't find your survival kit, consider it gone)
