# Shipwreck on Guyoth IV

By Gianluca Girelli

## Background

The events that took place on the Discovery put your safety at serious risk and, potentially, could have led to the arrival on Earth of an extraterrestrial pathogen capable of wiping out human life on the planet.

However, the virus samples recovered from the Discovery, identified and analysed using data extracted from the ship's computer and the Captain's log, were enough to pinpoint the virus's origin: Guyoth IV, a small, Earth-like planet still undergoing geological evolution. As a veteran of such missions, you have been tasked with travelling to the Guyoth system to gather further data useful to complete the synthesis of a vaccine, to be used should the need arise.

You arrived with a small team. Once in orbit, you left them aboard the mothership for support and began your descent in a shuttle. Although you had carefully planned the descent, a sudden shift in weather, driven by the turbulent nature of Guyoth IV's atmosphere, pushed you into a storm zone with intense electrical activity. Struck by lightning and with your systems failing, you had no choice but to pull the ejection lever and entrust your life to your parachute.

After what felt like an eternity, the winds carried you toward a wooded area, where your parachute became snagged in the canopy of the tallest trees. While you thank your lucky stars for having survived, you look down with apprehension at the ground many metres below. Find a way to get down, complete your mission by recovering more samples, and make your way back to the ship.

This is a traditional text adventure that was written for The BDB Project. The game is hosted at [Shipwreck on Guyoth IV](https://bdb-project.itch.io/shipwreck-on-guyoth-iv).
